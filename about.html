Our Beginning

From a small atelier to a beloved name

Aliya began in 1998 as a six-seat workshop behind our family's fabric store. One bench, two karigars, and a simple promise: every gram of gold accounted for, every stone honestly graded, every customer treated like family.

A quarter-century later, that promise hasn't changed. We still hand-finish our pieces in-house, still publish our rates daily, and still call every customer personally before their order ships.

What makes Aliya different

We believe jewellery should feel personal, not pressured. That's why our offers are flexible, our pricing is transparent, and our collection is curated — you'll never find 4,000 near-identical SKUs here, only pieces our team would wear themselves.
